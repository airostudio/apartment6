Cascade Apartment 6

Mt Baw Baw Alpine Resort, Victoria, Australia (47 reviews) Apartment

Welcome to Cascade Apartment 6, a fully self-contained mountain retreat at Mt Baw Baw Alpine Resort — Victoria's closest ski resort to Melbourne, just 2.5 hours from the city. Situated conveniently on the ground floor, the apartment is approximately 50 metres from the main day car park, making it ideal when you're loaded up with ski gear or mountain bikes.

The bedroom features a queen size bed with two single overhead bunk beds, while the spacious living area has two sofa beds — meaning the apartment comfortably accommodates up to five guests. Gather around the gas log fire after a day on the snow, enjoy a home-cooked meal in the fully equipped kitchen, and unwind with a movie on the TV.

Whether you're visiting in winter for the snow and skiing, or in summer for the hiking, mountain biking and wildflower trails, Cascade Apartment 6 is your perfect alpine base. Note: pillows and quilts are provided for the bedroom; please BYO linen and towels or pre-book a linen hire pack from Guest Services.
