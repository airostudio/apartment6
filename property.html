Cascade Apartment 6

Mt Baw Baw Alpine Resort, VIC 3833 (47 reviews) Alpine Apartment

Welcome to Cascade Apartment 6, your perfect alpine escape at Mt Baw Baw Alpine Resort. Nestled within the Cascade Apartments complex, this beautifully appointed 3-bedroom apartment offers true ski-in/ski-out convenience — step straight from the slopes onto your doorstep after a day on the mountain.

The apartment sleeps up to 6 guests across three well-appointed bedrooms, making it ideal for families, groups of friends, or corporate retreats. Warm up by the cosy fireplace after a day of skiing or snowboarding, and enjoy spectacular mountain views from the balcony as the sun sets over the alpine landscape.

Fully self-contained with a complete kitchen, washer/dryer, dishwasher, and free on-site parking, Cascade Apartment 6 has everything you need for a comfortable mountain stay. Whether you're visiting for the winter snow season or exploring the resort's year-round hiking and nature trails, this is your home away from home in the Victorian Alps.
