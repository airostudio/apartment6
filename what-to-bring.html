What to Bring

Your packing guide for a hassle-free stay at Cascade Apartment 6, Mt Baw Baw

Important: BYO Linen & Towels

Linen and towels are not provided — please remember to bring your own for every guest.

  • Bed sheets and pillowcases for each bed you plan to use
  • Bath towels and hand towels for each guest
  • Beach/pool towels if needed

Provided by the Apartment

The following bedding is supplied for all beds — no need to bring these:

  • Doonas (duvets) for all beds
  • Blankets for all beds
  • Pillows for all beds

Full Packing Checklist

Linen & Bedding

  • Fitted sheets and flat sheets (per bed used)
  • Pillowcases (2 per person)
  • Bath towels (1–2 per guest)
  • Hand towels
  • Doonas, blankets & pillows are supplied ✓

Clothing & Warmth

  • Thermal base layers (top & bottom)
  • Warm mid-layer fleece or jumper
  • Waterproof/windproof outer jacket
  • Waterproof pants / ski pants
  • Warm socks (merino wool recommended)
  • Beanie, gloves and neck gaiter/scarf
  • Casual / après-ski clothes
  • Warm pyjamas

Snow & Ski Gear

  • Ski boots or snowboard boots
  • Skis / snowboard (or hire at resort)
  • Ski helmet (highly recommended)
  • Snow goggles
  • Waterproof ski gloves
  • Ski socks (thin & warm)
  • Ski hire available at Mt Baw Baw resort

Food & Kitchen

  • Groceries & snacks for your stay
  • Drinks (alcoholic & non-alcoholic)
  • Breakfast items (cereals, bread, eggs)
  • Cooking oils, condiments & spices
  • Kitchen is fully equipped — pots, pans, utensils, microwave all provided ✓
  • Stock up in town before heading up — limited supplies at resort

Toiletries & Personal

  • Shampoo, conditioner & body wash
  • Toothbrush & toothpaste
  • Sunscreen (UV is intense at altitude)
  • Lip balm with SPF
  • Moisturiser (cold air dries skin quickly)
  • Any prescription medications
  • Hair dryer provided in the apartment ✓

Entertainment & Extras

  • DVDs (DVD player & TV provided)
  • Card games, board games, books
  • Phone / camera chargers
  • Power bank for mountain activities
  • Cash for resort facilities & hire
  • TV & DVD player provided in living area ✓
  • CD/radio player also available ✓

Please Note

  • No pets — the apartment is strictly pet-free
  • No smoking inside the apartment or on the grounds
  • Linen & towels are not supplied — BYO for all guests
  • Maximum 6 guests — please respect the occupancy limit
  • Check-in from 3:00 PM, check-out by 11:00 AM
