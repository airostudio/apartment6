How to Connect Your Platforms

Airbnb

  1. 1 Log in to Airbnb and go to your listing's Calendar page.
  2. 2 Click "Availability settings" then "Sync calendars".
  3. 3 Click "Import calendar" and paste your iCal Export URL from above.
  4. 4 Copy your Airbnb iCal export link and paste it into the Airbnb import field above, then click "Save & Sync".

Booking.com

  1. 1 Log in to the Booking.com Extranet and navigate to Calendar.
  2. 2 Select "Export calendar" to get your Booking.com iCal URL.
  3. 3 Paste that URL into the Booking.com import field above and click "Save & Sync".
  4. 4 Under "Import calendar", paste your iCal Export URL from above so Booking.com can read your availability.
