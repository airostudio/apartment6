iCal Sync

Sync your availability with external booking platforms

How calendar import works: paste the iCal link from Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO or Google Calendar. Their reservations are imported as blocked dates, so those nights can no longer be booked on this site. Feeds refresh automatically (at most every 10 minutes, and again just before a guest pays) or when you click Sync Now. If a provider can't be reached, the dates already imported stay blocked.

Export is not available yet. Bookings made on this site are not published back to Airbnb/Booking.com, so also block those dates on the other platforms until export is added.
