Inside Cascade Apartment 6

A cosy alpine retreat with everything you need for the perfect mountain getaway

Your Alpine Retreat

Cascade Apartment 6 is a fully self-contained one-bedroom apartment at Mt Baw Baw Alpine Resort, Victoria's closest ski resort to Melbourne. Situated on the ground floor for easy access, the apartment is just 50 metres from the main day car park.

The bedroom features a queen size bed with two single overhead bunk beds, while the living area includes two sofa beds — comfortably sleeping up to five guests in total. Gather around the gas log fire after a day on the snow or trails, enjoy a home-cooked meal in the fully equipped kitchen, and unwind in front of the TV.

Whether you're here for winter skiing, summer hiking, or a quiet mountain escape, Cascade Apartment 6 has everything you need for a relaxing, hassle-free stay. Note: linen and towels are BYO or available to hire from Guest Services.
